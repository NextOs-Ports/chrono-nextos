Esta pasta recebe os assets do jogo (assets/ do seu APK) na primeira abertura.

transacional. Coloque o SEU APK em ../gamedata/.

This folder receives the game assets (your APK's assets/) on the first launch.
Do not put anything here by hand: the installer publishes the payload
transactionally. Put YOUR APK in ../gamedata/ instead.
